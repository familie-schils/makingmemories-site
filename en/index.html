The travel notebook, made digital

We all know it: the notebook in the glovebox. Every fill-up, every campsite and every ice cream along the way, neatly jotted down. And then, a year later: where exactly did we stay on the Moselle? What did that campsite cost? It's in there somewhere, on a page with a coffee stain.

Making Memories does exactly the same, but on your phone. You note things down on the road in a few taps, the app adds up the miles and the costs for you, and the photos belong right there with them. After the trip you don't have a scribbled-full notebook, but a lovely account you'll still enjoy opening years later.

What you keep track of

Overnight stays and breaks
Place and country, how many nights and the price per night or stay, your rating and what you thought of it.
Odometer
At departure and at every arrival. The app suggests the last reading, and you adjust it.
Costs and fill-ups
Fuel, overnight stays and other spending, totalled for each trip.
Maintenance
When your vehicle was serviced and what was done.
Photos
Saved for every stop in print quality, ready to be turned into a photo album or travel book.
Trip report
Every finished trip as a PDF with photos, or as an Excel file.

Best of all

Not with the whole world. Only with the people you choose.

Your trips are yours. Making Memories is not a social network: no followers, no public profiles, no reviews from strangers and no map where everyone can see your favourite spots.

You choose your friends, and for each place or route you decide whether they may see it. You also decide who joins your travel log, such as your partner, your children or your travel companions: view only, or help keep it up to date.

Your data is kept on servers in the European Union, in Ireland.

No automatic GPS tracking: the app only knows what you enter.

No advertising and no tracking cookies.

And there's more

  • Tips from your friends

    See where your friends stayed and what they thought of it. Search by country, region or place and find your next spot.

  • Save and share routes

    Keep your GPX routes in one place and share them with friends, ready for their satnav.

  • Club rides

    For motorbike and cycling clubs: plan rides, sign up, share out tasks and afterwards put together a ride report with photos.

  • Your vehicle's handbook

    How does the boiler work, where is that fuse? Write it down once, with photos, for everyone who comes along.

  • Days out

    A walk, a museum or a café terrace during your trip: those days out belong in your account too.

  • Speaks your language

    Rides on the motorbike, boat trips in nautical miles, hikes with your step count. The app adapts to what you do.

  • Easy to read and use for everyone

    In your profile you can choose larger text and a colour-blind-friendly display. If you travel in a wheelchair, you note for each place how accessible it is: steps, toilet, shower, ramp or lift, parking.
