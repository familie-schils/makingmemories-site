For guests: how to join

  1. Scan the QR code or open the linkYou get the link from the party host, or you scan the QR code on the poster. You don't need to install anything.
  2. Choose a nicknameA nickname is enough. You don't create an account and you don't give an email address.
  3. Take a photo or upload moreTap the big button and your camera opens: the photo is in the album straight away. With "Upload more photos" you pick several photos from your gallery.
  4. Look at the albumSee the photos of all the guests in the order of the evening. Turn on notifications if you want to know when there are new photos.

Privacy

Your nickname and photos are only visible to the guests and the party host of that party. Not to the whole world, and not in a public profile.

They are deleted 30 days after the party. Is there a photo of you that you don't want in the album? Ask the party host: they can remove any photo straight away. Only share photos of people who are happy for you to do so.

For businesses: venue, photographer or DJ

With a subscription you create parties yourself. Guests join with a link or QR code. You get:

  • Photo screen

    You set up the photo screen in the room: the guests' photos appear live on a big screen.

  • Poster with QR code

    A poster to print: guests scan the QR code and join straight away.

  • Appoint party hosts

    You appoint the party host: the person whose party it is. They get their own personal link to manage the album.

The photos belong to the party host, not to the business

As a business you don't see the guests' photos. The party host manages the album: they see the photos, can remove any photo, make the photo book as a PDF and can also set up the photo screen.
