Parties and events

All the photos of the party, in one album.

After a party, the best photos are scattered across dozens of phones. With Making Memories they end up together in one shared album. Guests join with a link or a QR code: no app and no account needed.

For venues, photographers and organisers I'm a guest

For guests: how to join

  1. Scan the QR code or open the linkYou get the link from the organiser, or you scan the QR code on the poster. You don't need to install anything.
  2. Choose a nicknameA nickname is enough. You don't create an account and you don't give an email address.
  3. Take a photo or upload moreTap the big button and your camera opens: the photo is in the album straight away. With "Upload more photos" you pick several photos from your gallery.
  4. Look at the albumSee the photos of all the guests in the order of the evening. Turn on notifications if you want to know when there are new photos.

Privacy

Your nickname and photos are only visible to the guests and the organiser of that party. Not to the whole world, and not in a public profile.

They are deleted 30 days after the party. Is there a photo of you that you don't want in the album? Ask the organiser: they can remove any photo straight away. Only share photos of people who are happy for you to do so.

For businesses: venue, photographer or organiser

With a subscription you create parties yourself and let your guests join with a link or QR code. You get:

  • Photo screen

    The guests' photos appear live on a big screen in the room.

  • Shared album

    All photos from all guests in one place, in the order of the evening. You can remove any photo straight away.

  • Photo book as PDF

    After the party you turn the album into a party book as a PDF.

  • Poster with QR code

    A poster to print: guests scan the QR code and join straight away.

  • Hosts

    Appoint hosts for the party: they get their own personal link to join in.
