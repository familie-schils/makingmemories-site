Privacy statement

This is a translation for convenience. The Dutch version is legally binding.

Last updated on 26 November 2026.

In brief

  • We only keep what you put into the app yourself: your trips, places, costs, photos and routes.
  • No GPS tracking, no advertising, no tracking cookies and no public profiles.
  • The weather in the app comes from an external service. For this, a location accurate to about 1 km is sent, even if you don't tap anything.
  • If you use a wheelchair and tick that in your profile, this is health data. Only you can see it.
  • We don't sell anything and don't build profiles of you.
  • Only you and the people you choose can see your data.
  • Your data is kept on servers in the EU, in Ireland.
  • You can delete your account and everything that goes with it yourself in the app.
  • At a party: the venue does not see your photos, and everything is deleted 30 days after the party.
  • Questions? Email info@makingmemories.be.

Who is responsible

Making Memories is run by Matthias Schils, Lanaken, Belgium. Send questions about your data to info@makingmemories.be.

Which data we keep
  • Your account: email address, display name and an encrypted password that we cannot read.
  • What you enter yourself: vehicles, trips, overnight stays, breaks, days out, odometer readings, fill-ups, costs, maintenance, notes and the names of travel companions.
  • Photos and routes (GPX) that you add. Photos are reduced in size; the location that was in the photo is lost in the process.
  • For each trip: whether you travel with a wheelchair and your pets (type and name). For a place you can optionally note how accessible it was for a wheelchair.
  • Friends, invitations (with the email address of the person you invite), clubs and club rides.
  • Whether you have the free trial or full access.
  • If you request an invitation through this website: your name, email address, who told you about us, what you travel with or details about your party or business, and your message. We delete that request after 180 days at the most.

The app does not track your location in the background and does not store your location in your account. What the app passes on for the weather is described under "Weather and places".

Wheelchair: health data

In your profile you can tick "I use a wheelchair". That says something about your health and falls under the special categories of data in the GDPR. That is why we only keep it if you tick it yourself: that tick is your explicit consent. We only use it to switch on place filters by default and to show wheelchair information.

  • This profile data is kept in a separate table that only you can read and change. Friends and the members of your vehicle cannot see it.
  • If you untick it, we delete it. If you delete your account, it disappears too.
  • You can withdraw your consent at any time by unticking it; anything that happened before remains lawful.

For a trip it is different: if you tick "with wheelchair" there, the people who may see that trip (the members of your vehicle and friends you share the trip with) see a badge on the trip. The same goes for pets and for the accessibility you fill in for a place. If you don't want that, leave it empty.

Why we use it

Only to make the travel log work: showing your data to you and to the people you share it with, and sending emails that belong to your account (such as an invitation or a link for a new password). That is necessary for the agreement with you. We use a request for an invitation only to be able to invite you, because you asked for it yourself. We use the email address of someone you invite only to send that invitation (legitimate interest); anyone who doesn't want that can ask us to delete it.

Who can see your data
  • The members of your vehicle, according to their role (view only or help keep it up to date). Costs are visible to all members.
  • Your friends: only what you share with friends.
  • Club members: what you share with a club ride, and your sign-up.
  • The administrator of Making Memories, only when necessary to fix a problem.
Service providers and transfers outside the EU

These services work for us and may only use your data for that purpose:

  • Supabase: database, photos, routes and login, in Ireland (EU).
  • Brevo (France): emails that belong to your account, and the message that lets us know you requested an invitation (the request itself is stored by Supabase). OVH (France): forwarding mail to info@makingmemories.be.
  • Cloudflare and GitHub (United States): serving the app and this website; they see your IP address in the process.

Where data may leave the EU (Cloudflare, GitHub, Supabase's subcontractors), this is done under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

The app also loads the map from OpenStreetMap and program code from jsDelivr and cdnjs. They see your IP address, but receive nothing from your account. For a new route, the app sends only the start and end point to OpenStreetMap, to look up the place name. With "Share on WhatsApp" you open WhatsApp yourself.

Weather and places: what the app sends out

For campervan and boat, the app shows the weather. It comes from the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway, api.met.no; licence CC BY 4.0). Your device asks that service for the weather directly, so they see your IP address.

  • When: automatically when you open the home screen, as a rule at most every 15 minutes, even if you don't tap anything.
  • What: a single coordinate, rounded to two decimals (accurate to about 1 km). No name, no email address, nothing from your account.
  • Which place: first a place you chose yourself, then your current position (only if your device has already given the app permission; the app does not ask for it itself, unless you choose "Weather here"), then the overnight stay where you are now, then a planned overnight stay, then the last place used. For an overnight stay, the app looks up the coordinate: the place name, region and country of that stay are sent automatically to Nominatim (OpenStreetMap), which sees your IP address.
  • On your device: the forecast and the last place used (rounded) are kept by the app in your browser storage, not on our servers.
  • Windy: the weather sheet contains a link to Windy. Only when you tap it does Windy receive a coordinate (three decimals) and your IP address.

If you search for a place yourself or open "Nearby", that also goes to external services, only after you tap and only with the search text or the coordinate: Nominatim (searching places), Photon by Komoot (fallback when searching), Wikidata and Wikivoyage (Wikimedia, United States; sights and travel guide) and Waymarked Trails (cycling and hiking routes). They see your IP address and nothing from your account.

Cookies and storage on your device

No tracking cookies and no analytics services. The app only stores what is needed on your device: staying logged in, remembering your choices and opening the app without a connection. No consent is needed for that.

Security

All traffic is encrypted. The database only lets everyone see what they have access to, and photos and routes are kept in protected storage, not publicly on the internet.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. You delete your account yourself via More › My profile › Delete account, or you ask us to at info@makingmemories.be (it is then done within 30 days). Everything of yours then disappears, including your photos. A vehicle or club that still has other members remains for them. Backups are overwritten after 30 days at the most. What we are legally required to keep, such as invoices, we keep for as long as the law requires.

Your rights and complaints

You can view your data, have it corrected or deleted, and ask for a copy (you can also download a trip yourself as a PDF or Excel file). You can also object or ask us to restrict processing. Email info@makingmemories.be; you will get an answer within a month.

Not satisfied? You can lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit), Drukpersstraat 35, 1000 Brussels.

Other people's data

If you put photos or names of other people in the app, make sure they agree; for children, ask their parents. You can have your own account from the age of 13.

When you join a party

If you join through a party's QR code or link, we only keep what you enter and upload: your nickname, your photos and possibly a short text. You don't need an account. Your device gets an anonymous login that only works for this party.

  • Who sees what? The other guests and the party organiser (for example the couple or the person celebrating) see your photos with your nickname. If the organiser uses a photo screen, your photos can appear there too, unless you tick "Not on the screen". The venue or business that created the party does not see your photos or nickname, only how many people take part.
  • How long? You can upload photos until shortly after the party. 30 days after the party we automatically delete all photos and data of the party. The organiser can download an album or photo book beforehand and keep it.
  • Deleting: you can delete your own photos. The organiser can delete any photo. If there is a photo of you that you don't want, ask the organiser or email info@makingmemories.be.
  • Who decides? The party organiser decides what the photos are for. Making Memories stores and shows them on their behalf and never uses them for anything else: no advertising, no selling and no AI training.
  • Only share photos of people who agree. You can take part yourself from age 16.
Changes

If this statement changes, we will update the date at the top. We will announce important changes first in the app or by email.
